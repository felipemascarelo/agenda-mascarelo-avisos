Gatilho dos avisos (Cloudflare Worker)
1) GitHub > Settings > Developer settings > Fine-grained tokens > Generate: repositório agenda-mascarelo-avisos, permissão Actions: Read and write. Copie o token.
2) Cloudflare > Workers & Pages > Create > Worker > cole gatilho.js > Deploy.
3) Settings > Variables and Secrets > adicione o segredo GH_TOKEN = token do passo 1.
4) Settings > Triggers > Cron Triggers > adicione */5 * * * *
